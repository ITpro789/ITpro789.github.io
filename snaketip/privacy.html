Snaketip privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Snaketip is a puzzle game published by SKP Studios ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your progress, settings, statistics and purchases are stored on your device. If you enter a player name, it stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is described under Usage statistics, Advertising and Purchases below.

Backups

On Android, your progress may be included in your Google device backup so it can move to a new phone. This is controlled by your device settings.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To see which levels are too hard and to fix crashes, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They record how the game is played (for example which level was started, won, failed or left, how long it took, and which world you chose next), crash details, the device model and operating system, and an app instance ID created by Firebase. They do not record your name, email or player name, and we do not use this data for advertising.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Usage stats and crash reports. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland, the choices you make in Google's consent form also apply to Analytics.

Google's policy: Privacy and security in Firebase.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from your IP address, and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland we ask for your consent first using Google's consent form; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. On iPhone we ask for permission before any tracking. Ads are muted, and you can remove interstitial ads with a one-off purchase.

Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, never your payment details.

Children

Snaketip is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data erases everything the game stores. Uninstalling the game also removes it. Usage statistics are kept by Google Analytics for 2 months and then deleted; to ask us to delete them sooner, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
